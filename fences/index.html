The short answer

Updated November 2026

Which fence is best for a NYC home?

It depends on the job. Aluminum for front yards and pools: no rust, no upkeep. Vinyl for backyard privacy. Cedar for gardens with a warmer look. Steel or wrought iron for brownstones, chain link for lots and play areas. Most NYC homes are limited to 4 ft in front and 6 ft at the sides and rear.

Choosing a system for a NYC yard. For permits and height limits, see NYC fence rules.

Which fence lasts longest in New York?

–

Aluminum, steel and vinyl last decades. Chain link lasts 20+ years, cedar 15–20 years if sealed every few years.

What is the cheapest fence to install?

+

Chain link is usually the lowest cost per foot, then wood. Aluminum and vinyl cost more up front but need little or no upkeep.

Which fence gives the most privacy?

+

A solid vinyl privacy fence or a board-on-board wood fence. Chain link with privacy slats is the budget option.

What fence is best for a pool?

+

Aluminum is the usual choice: it does not rust near water and is easy to build to pool code with self-closing, self-latching gates.

Can I mix two fence types on one property?

+

Yes, and it is common: an open aluminum fence in front and a vinyl or wood privacy fence along the back. We match colors and heights so it looks planned.
