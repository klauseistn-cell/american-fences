NYC fence rules,
handled for you.

Zoning and DOB rules decide how tall your fence can be. We check them on every estimate, so you never build twice.

6 ft

No DOB permit needed for fences up to 6 ft on one- and two-family homes.

1 RCNY §101-14

4 ft

Front-yard limit in lower-density R1–R5 districts. Corner lots allow 6 ft on the street side.

ZR §23-44

10 ft

General maximum. Taller or non-residential fences need a DOB PW1 application, which we file.

Questions,
answered.

How much does a fence cost in New York?

–

Price depends on material, height, length, gates and ground conditions. After a free on-site measure you get a written, line-item quote, usually the same week.

Do you repair or replace old fences?

+

Both. We fix posts, panels, gates and hardware, and when a repair is not worth it we tell you and quote a replacement.

Do you install gates as well as fences?

+

Yes. Walk gates, driveway gates and openers are built by the same crew, in the same style and color as the fence. See Gates & automation for the options.

Do you take on commercial projects?

+

Yes. We fence job sites, lots and buildings for developers, property managers and businesses, with a COI on request. See Commercial for details.
