The short answer

Updated November 2026

How tall can a fence be in NYC?

In most NYC residential districts a fence can be up to 4 ft in the front yard and up to 6 ft in side and rear yards. Corner lots can usually go to 6 ft on the side street but must stay low at the corner. Fences above 6 ft generally need a DOB permit; pools need a barrier at least 4 ft high.

Sources:

NYC Zoning Resolution §23-44

·

NYC Department of Buildings

·

NYC Landmarks Preservation Commission

·

Fence rules,
answered.

The questions homeowners ask us most on the measure. Rules can differ by zoning district, so we always confirm for your exact address.

How tall can a front yard fence be in NYC?

–

In most lower-density residential districts (R1–R5), up to 4 ft in the front yard. Higher-density districts can differ, so we check the zoning map for your lot.

How tall can a backyard fence be?

+

Up to 6 ft along side and rear lot lines in most residential districts. That covers a standard privacy fence.

Do I need a permit to build a fence in NYC?

+

Usually not for a fence within the height limits on your own lot. Taller fences and some locations need approval; we check your address first.

What are the rules for a corner lot?

+

Corner lots can have extra limits near the corner so drivers can see. We check your lot before choosing the height and layout.

What fence do I need around a pool?

+

A barrier of the required height with self-closing, self-latching gates and no easy footholds. We build pool fences to code.

Can I build a fence right on the property line?

+

Yes, but you need to know exactly where the line is. If there is any doubt, a survey settles it before we dig.

Does a historic district change anything?

+

Yes. Work visible from the street in a historic district can need Landmarks approval. We check your address first.

Who checks the rules for my address?

+

We do. On the free measure we check the zoning, the lot and any special district for your address.
